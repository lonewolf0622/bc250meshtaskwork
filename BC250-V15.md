# BC250 v15 experimental checkpoint

Based on v14 commit79748a70251a0142593014e7cf2985eb20d5429f, incorporating the exact vertex-packing candidate tested on two independent fresh boots. Preserves the candidate's compaction and diagnostic gates without changing their defaults.

Packed triangle exports are limited to GFX10 single-wave POS-only triangles, no multiview/attribute ring/scratch outputs, with3*max_primitives<=max_vertices<=wave_size. This does not establish general mesh/task shader support.

Validated candidate library before this versioned rebuild:28d14a461f8d938de415119454a5b0757a5f951f78e9f2b243245b2019c01d88. Two mask5 visible-image trials produced508 red pixels/zero analytic violations, then completed60sec retention and300sec post-cleanup observation. These are bounded tests, not long-term stability proof. No new GPU trial of the versioned rebuild is implied.

Reproduction options: BC250_EXPERIMENTAL_CULL_COMPACT=true, BC250_EXPERIMENTAL_PACK_TRIANGLE_VERTICES=true, MESA_SHADER_CACHE_DISABLE=true, RADV_PERFTEST=mesh, RADV_DEBUG=syncshaders, BC250_EXPERIMENTAL_POST_MESH_VGT_FLUSH=1. Avoid reused application pipeline caches: compiler experiment flags are not keyed. These options are experimental, not a recommended general Steam launch command.

BC250_DIAGNOSTIC_OMIT_LAUNCH must remain unset for real rendering. Do not enable raster-discard/zero-scissor/skip-draw harness diagnostics in rendering tests. Failed preload and primitive-first changes are not included. The retained post-VGT-flush diagnostic is not independently a proven fix.

Remaining: broader masks/shared-vertex fixtures, query and task integration, attributes/multiwave support, production compiler options and cache-key separation, longer stability/performance validation. Keep v14 and existing working launchers unchanged. Do not install this checkpoint globally or claim unsupported shaders safe.
